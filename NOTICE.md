# Third-party design references

This project is not yet released. The following sources are being used during
development and must remain attributed in any derived release.

- `ssbb/tako`, copyright 2023 ssbb, MIT License. Revision A derives its EC
  sensor footprint, sensing topology, logical matrix, and mechanical stack
  constraints from Tako Rev1.
- `davidphilipbarr/Sweep`, copyright 2020 David Barr, Solderpad Hardware
  License 2.1 (with its stated Apache-2.0 option). Revision A derives its key
  stagger and general silhouette from the Sweep family.
- QMK source by ssbb and Cipulot is GPL-licensed per its file headers. Any
  copied or modified firmware files will retain those headers and be
  distributed under compatible terms.

Exact commits are recorded in `references/reference-audit.md`.
